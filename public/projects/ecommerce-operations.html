Ecommerce Operations & Shopify Store Management

A comprehensive ecommerce operations support project focused on streamlining Shopify store management, maintaining accurate product listings, managing orders and inventory, and delivering a smooth customer experience through organized digital workflows.

ClientSample Project
TimelineOngoing
OutcomeOrganized store operations and improved ecommerce workflow management.

01The Problem

Managing an ecommerce store involves multiple daily responsibilities, from maintaining product listings and monitoring inventory to processing customer orders and handling inquiries. Without organized workflows, these tasks can become time-consuming and lead to delays, inaccurate product information, and inconsistent customer experiences.

02The Solution

Provided structured ecommerce virtual assistance to support day-to-day Shopify store operations.

  • Shopify product listing and catalog management
  • Product descriptions, pricing, and image updates
  • Inventory and stock availability monitoring
  • Order processing and fulfillment coordination
  • Customer inquiries and email support
  • Returns, exchanges, and order follow-ups
  • Order tracking and delivery updates
